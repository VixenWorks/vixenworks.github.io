"$4,997 a month is a big commitment when we're still early."

It is. And one wrong architectural decision costs you 10x that in developer time, rebuild cost, and missed runway. It's whether you can afford not to have senior technical oversight at the most critical juncture of your company's life. You can cancel any time. "I already have developers. Why do I need a fractional CTO on top of that?"

Your developers build what they're told. They optimise for code, not for commercial outcomes. A fractional CTO asks the questions your developers don't: Are we building the right thing? Is this architecture going to hold at 100x load? Is this tech stack going to look credible in a seed round? Your team executes. We make sure they're executing on the right things in the right way.

"How do I know 20 hours a month is actually enough?"

For most early-stage startups, 20 focused hours of senior CTO work per month is transformative. That's weekly meetings, active technical oversight, architecture input, investor prep, and strategic direction. It's not a full-time engagement, but it's structured to create the most leverage exactly where you need it. We'll be transparent with you if your situation genuinely requires the Scale plan instead.

"We're moving fast. Onboarding someone new will slow us down."

Onboarding takes days, not weeks. We're built to ramp quickly and add value from the first session, not in month three after a lengthy knowledge-transfer exercise. The real speed risk is continuing without senior technical leadership and discovering, six months from now, that you've been building fast in the wrong direction.

"I'm not technical. Will I even understand what you're doing?"

This is exactly who the Start Up Tier is designed for. Every recommendation comes with a plain-English explanation of the what, why, and what it means for your business. You will always know what decisions are being made and why, and you will always be in control. We don't build a black box around your own technology.

"What if I outgrow this plan?"

That's a good problem to have, and it means we've done our job.
